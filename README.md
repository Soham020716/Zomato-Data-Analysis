# Zomato Food Delivery & Business Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Short Description

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Zomato food delivery data and generate business insights related to orders, revenue, delivery performance, customer demand, locations, cuisine preferences, profitability, and growth opportunities.

---

## 2. Tech Stack

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and business KPIs
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships and analytical structure
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data source and preprocessing

---

## 3. Features & Highlights

### Business Problem

Food delivery platforms generate large amounts of data related to orders, locations, cuisines, delivery times, revenue, and customer demand. Analyzing this data manually makes it difficult to identify high-performing areas, delivery problems, popular cuisines, and potential business opportunities.

### Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this Power BI project is to transform raw food delivery data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps identify:

- High-demand locations and micro-markets
- Popular cuisine categories
- Order and revenue trends
- Weekday vs weekend ordering patterns
- Delivery performance and delays
- Cancellation and on-time delivery performance
- Profitability across different locations
- Traffic impact on delivery time
- Potential areas for opening new shops
- Cuisine-wise business opportunities

### Key Dashboard Sections

#### 1. Executive & Time Analytics
- Total Orders
- Total Revenue
- Average Delivery Time
- Cancellation Rate
- Profit Margin
- Daily order trends
- Hourly order throughput
- Average delivery time by hour

#### 2. Location & Intelligence
- Top zones and micro-markets by demand
- Zone-wise order analysis
- Revenue and profitability analysis
- Identification of high-performing locations
- Bandra and Andheri identified as major demand-generating areas

#### 3. Food & Cuisine Analysis
- Cuisine-wise order distribution
- Cuisine contribution by time of day
- Cuisine profitability comparison
- Cuisine strategy matrix
- Identification of the most popular cuisine categories

#### 4. Delivery & Growth Optimization
- On-time vs delayed orders
- Rider efficiency
- Traffic impact on delivery
- Average delivery delay
- Current shops vs recommended new shop locations
- Identification of potential growth areas

---

## 4. Key Business Insights

- The dashboard analyzes approximately **5,000 orders** and around **2M in revenue**.
- **Chinese cuisine** has the highest number of orders, followed by **North Indian cuisine**.
- **Bandra and Andheri** are among the highest-demand micro-markets.
- Delivery performance and traffic have a significant impact on operational efficiency.
- The dashboard highlights areas with high demand that can be considered for future expansion.
- Hourly and daily trends help identify peak ordering periods and optimize delivery operations.
